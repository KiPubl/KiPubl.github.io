About Us

Critters of the Forgotten Realms is a museum dedicated to showcasing the diverse and fantastical creatures of the Forgotten Realms. From the tiniest of beasts to the largest of dragons, we are dedicated to sharing the diverse ecosystems and its inhabitants that populate the Realms.

Our Mission

Critters of the Forgotten Realms aims to inspire understanding and respect for all creatures, from the mundane to the fantastical.


Our Vision

We envision a world where our communities can thrive respectfully among creatures of all types.


Our Values

  • Respect: In cultivating a culture of respect for all creatures, inspiring respect for all
  • Diversity: Celebrating the unique characteristics of each creature to recognize and value the diversity of our communities
  • Courage: Standing up for the rights and well-being of all creatures
  • Collaboration: Working together to create a more inclusive environment for all
  • Community: Building connections and relationships within our diverse community, better connecting ourselves with the greater world
